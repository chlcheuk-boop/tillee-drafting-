# Brand story and voice (PROPOSED)

> This is a proposal for founder review. It is not an approved rule. It must be reconciled with `Tillee_Brand_Guidelines.md` and the source slogan "Take Control of Your Taxes with Tillee", neither of which was available here.

## Idea: "Clarity, without the tax panic"

Tillee is the capable companion who makes sense of confusing tax language before it turns into a surprise. Tillee is on your side: observant, practical, lightly cheeky, composed.

The mascot is a small receipt with a ledger for a belly. It's **paperwork that's on your side**. The joke is always that paperwork and jargon are absurd. It is never the user's income, debt, family, knowledge or mistakes.

### Story arc through the checkup

| Moment | What the story says | Mascot |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | "Let's make your taxes a little less mysterious." Purpose, then what's needed | Greeting (one wave) |
| Bring info in | We'll do the typing where we can; you check our work | none |
| Questions | One thing at a time, with the reason available on demand | Quiet beside the form on desktop; explaining pose inside "Why we ask" |
| Section break | Acknowledge effort, preview what's next | Small celebration |
| Processing | Show what's really happening, nothing invented | Thinking |
| Failure | Plain facts, nothing lost, how to retry | Supportive |
| Result | Calm and literal, whether a refund or a bill | Reassuring |

## Voice

- **Plain English first**, with jargon explained where it comes up ("Look for 'YTD gross' on your latest pay stub").
- **Warm and direct.** Short sentences. Contractions are fine.
- **Wit in small doses**, and only in low-stakes moments (welcome, section breaks, an apology that's "on us, not you").
- **Literal for** consent, privacy, errors, money amounts and result caveats.
- **Never:** guilt, streak pressure, childish rewards, certainty we don't have, implying a professional reviewed the answers, treating a refund as a win or as "savings".

| Context | Use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | "Let's make your taxes a little less mysterious." | "Taxes are easy! Let's play!" |
| Help | "This helps us understand income your employer may not know about." | "Don't forget your side hustle, tax genius." |
| Transition | "That's the basics. A few details about your household next." | Applause after every answer |
| Upload failure | "That file didn't go through. Try again, or choose another file." | "Oops, you messed up!" |
| Completion | "Your checkup is saved. Here's what comes next." | "You're guaranteed to save money." |
| Owing money | "You may owe $X–$Y when you file." plus a caveat | Confetti, scolding, alarm |

## Tax-language guardrails used in the copy

- A refund is overpayment, not savings. Changing withholding changes timing, not total tax.
- Filing status is not relationship status. The filing question says so.
- "People who may be your dependents". Eligibility is never assumed.
- Every amount is labelled with its period (year to date, or expected for the full year) and source plus as-of date.
